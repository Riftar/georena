# GeoRena roadmap

## Playable modes

- Racing time trials with checkpoints, lap timing, and split times.
- Timed FPS depot rounds with objective scoring.
- Item spawns and mode-specific power-ups.

## Arena and vehicle customization

- Set the number and difficulty of turns in racing arenas.
- Build racing and FPS arenas from paths or top-down images, with elevation.
- Save and reopen arena setups.
- Generate beach, mountain, and city arenas with weather and elemental effects.
- Customize car colors and decals.

## In-game UI and rewards

- Pause menus, controls, HUDs, and player avatars for both modes.
- Live scoreboards and end-of-round results.
- FPS achievement badges and Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Champion trophies.

## Game of the Day

- Play one shared racing or FPS arena each day.
- Retry the daily challenge and share your best lap time or objective score.
